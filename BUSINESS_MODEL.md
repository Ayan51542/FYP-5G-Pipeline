## 1. Company Overview

### 1.1 Problem Statement

The rollout of 5G networks and cognitive radio systems creates new vulnerabilities:
- **Jamming Attacks**: Malicious actors can inject high-power interference, disrupting communications
- **Economic Impact**: Telecommunications companies lose billions annually to spectrum attacks
- **Security Gap**: Current defenses are static and reactive, not adaptive
- **Regulatory Risk**: Governments mandate secure spectrum access (3GPP compliance)

### 1.2 Our Solution

SpectrumGuard provides an **adaptive, multi-layered anti-jamming platform** featuring:
- **M Variation**: Dynamic modulation switching (QAM-16 to QAM-256) based on channel conditions
- **ML-Based Detection**: Real-time jamming identification using trained neural networks
- **OFDM Frequency Diversity**: Spread-spectrum protection against narrowband interference
- **Error Correction**: Reed-Solomon + Turbo codes for resilient communication
- **Encrypted Authentication**: AES-GCM prevents spoofing and validates packets
